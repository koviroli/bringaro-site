02 / MARKET

Good food. A new home.

Have unopened surplus groceries? Eligible private individuals can offer them below their purchase cost. You don’t need a previous Bringaro delivery.

  • Unopened food in its original packaging
  • Private purchase proof and current product photos
  • Buyer pickup and direct payment

Purchase proof is not a food-safety or authenticity check. Additional rules apply to groceries from a Bringaro delivery.

A few things worth knowing.

Is this an on-demand delivery service?

Bringaro is built around trips people are already making. A match or delivery time is not guaranteed.

Who pays at the shop?

The shopper pays first. At handover, the customer reimburses the actual grocery cost plus the agreed reward. Bringaro does not process that payment.

Can I use Market without a delivery?

Yes. Market is designed for eligible private individuals with verified email and no active ban. Private purchase proof, current photos and below-cost pricing are required.
